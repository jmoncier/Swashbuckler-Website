Join the Swashbucklers!

Interested in becoming a member of the Swashbucklers? We are always looking for enthusiastic students who enjoy performing, meeting new people, and representing East Tennessee State University at events across the region.

No prior performance experience is required. Auditions are open to students who are willing to learn, participate, and work as part of a team.
