About the Swashbucklers

The Swashbucklers are an a cappella group at East Tennessee State University that brings students together through music, community, and performance. The Swashbucklers welcome everyone who shares a passion for singing and performing.

Explore our website to watch previous performances, discover upcoming events, meet our members, and learn how to become a Swashbuckler.
